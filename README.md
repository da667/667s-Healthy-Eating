## General Advice

This section details some general advice that has served me well on my journey towards better new nutrition. Hope you find it helpful!

### Keep a Food Journal

Best advice I can offer you is to keep track of everything ~~put in your mouth~~ eat. How you do that is entirely up to you, but here are some helpful guidelines:

- There are fitness apps for your smart phones. And they might even be pretty good, and have a huge database macros for various foods that might even make using them look attractive, and save you time. **Avoid Them**. Why? One word: **Enshitification**. Every single weight loss and tracking app I've seen that loads on a smart phone either starts off totally free and amazing, or starts off as freemium slop. And they both get progressively worse, as they get more and more attention. Attention attracts money. Money attracts shareholders. Shareholders want to extract more money out of a good thing, and end up trying to squeeze blood out of a stone, turning the entire application into a micro-transaction shitfest. See also: Every streaming service, and every single AAA gaming publisher. **Fuck em.** You don't need them.

- Use your preferred note-taking app instead. There's a good chance if you work in tech in a sedantary position, you have a note-taking or organizer app of some sort you use. Could be notepad. Could be word. Could be cloud solutions like Office365, Google Apps, Cryptpad, etc. For all I care, it could be VIM, EMACs, or your preferred IDE. Personally I use [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/download). Its free, its multi-platform, it supports markdown, has plugin support, and if you want to pay for it, can be made to synchronize with your other systems. Or, if you are the technomancer I know you are if you are reading this, *you'll find ways* to sync your files between devices for free -- VPN connection home to a fileshare with your Obsidian files, [syncthing](https://syncthing.net/), public cloud services, [self-hosted](https://owncloud.com/) [cloud services](https://cryptpad.fr), whatever. You and I both know that if there is a will, we find a way, because that's usually our job: Making shit work with hopes, prayers, and gratuitous amounts of elbow grease.
