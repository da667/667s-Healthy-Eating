# 667s-Healthy-Eating

## About Me
 Hi, I'm da_667. I work in tech, I'm 39 years old, I have high blood pressure (now controlled), Diabetes (Now also controlled), Chronic Kidney Disease (Stage 3, yup, controlled as well), and to top it all off, I'm morbidly obese (and unfucking that as well). I was 425lbs in December of 2025. 

As of mine writing this, it is April 4th, 2026. My blood pressure is well-controlled through diet and exercise and ranges in the low 100s Systolic over 60-70 Dialstolic, my [A1C](https://diabetes.org/about-diabetes/a1c), an extremely important measure of diabetic health, was 5.8 in February (Which, is *slightly* above what is considered normal), and I have lost 61 lbs Through diet, exercise, and medication. and yes, that includes the use of an [GLP-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GLP-1_receptor_agonist). My body was a desecrated, dilapidated temple. And to be fair, it *still* is. But I'm making strides.

Are you a technology professional in a mainly Sedentary job that sucks your soul out of your arse on a daily basis, leaving you a husk searching for the path of least resistance to provide your body with nutrition and caffience necessary to survive your burnout-filled days? Awesome, I'm just like you!(tm). I ate and drank whatever the fuck I wanted, and I have paid the price dearly. 

*"Why am I telling you all this?"*

Most normal people look at fat people with abject disgust. I've experienced it all my life. I have been obese all of my life. Children stare. Fully grown adults stare. Chunni-ass middle schoolers and high schoolers stare *and* laugh at you, because teenagers, hormones, and edginess. People of all kids say shit and assume they are out of earshot, but you *definitely* hear them. I've literally had a child walk up to my table at a restaurant with my wife, call me fat and ugly and just walked away like its the most natural thing in the world. The parents apologized profusely, I said it was fine, *"he's a child"*, but deep down, he learned that behavior *somewhere*, and never *unlearned* it enough for him to consider that what they did was anything resembling appropriate. the assumption is, if a personal is *that* morbidly obese, and *that* out of shape, than not only are the a huge waste of space, they're probably also stupid, entitled, and deserve your derision. The only difference between the average person and the obese is that an obese person's vices are on display for everyone to see.

All of this shit leaves deep psychological scars, and presents itself in the form of a vicious feedback loop. At least that was my experience. Now, I'm not here to have a pity party, or for us to compare scars, I'm just providing you some context. So in addition to being a healthy eating guide, let me also tell you that, mental health and general well-being tie in to healthy eating, in a big way. Your struggles are your own, I can't give you specific advice to overcome your problems, but in order to progress, you need to face or manage them. For me, it was acknowledging depression and taking the medication necessary to bring myself out of it, alongside all of the other lifestyle changes I made that are very slowly, but very steaily refurbishing this dilapidated bucket of bolts into something that will hopefully persist past its End of Life date. Like mission-critcal software and hardware at a Fortune 500 company.

I'm a self-published author. In the books I write about tech subjects, they contain knowledge about things I've experienced, and my will to share them with others in hopes that they learn from my experiences, use that information as a foundation, and expand upon it in their own journies towards their own destinations. In simpler terms, one of my favorite quote is:

*"If all my existence amounts to, is to serve as a warning to others, so be it"* or, something to that extent. I want you to learn from my experiences and my mistakes, and do better than I did. That's it. That's why I bothered writing this markdown.
